A tensor that exists

31 March 2025

A library without a tensor is a README. The 31st adds a rudimentary Tensor and a test that it can actually be built, then settles the habit of instantiating inside blocks so lifetime stays visible. Icons land. A write-up for the culture goes in, which is the honest version of a homepage: here is why this exists, here is how early it is.

The last note of the day is “should be failing, tomorrow.” That is the right posture. The type is clay. CPU and GPU are not even separate initializers yet. The vision is still larger than the file.
