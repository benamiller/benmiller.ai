Strides, almost always hidden

14 June 2026

Shape was already owned. Strides were the unfinished half. The 14th makes them explicit when a caller actually needs the layout, and hidden when they are just doing math. Tensor owns them. A few hours of broken intermediate states, a whoops, then “own strides.” That is HEAD.

The reason strides exist in a serious tensor library is reductions, broadcasts, views that do not copy. Axis sum was filed in April 2025 and never written. The layout is now ready for it. The op is not. Last push: 15 June 2026. Nothing since.
