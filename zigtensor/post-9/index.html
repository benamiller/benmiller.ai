The tensor owns its shape

7 February 2026

A tensor that does not own its stride and shape is a view into someone else’s story. The 7th puts both on the struct. Call sites stop passing a reference they should not have had to keep. Size-mismatch errors get one name so the API does not grow a dialect per function.

This is the setup for later strides work. You cannot hide a stride you do not own. You also cannot write an axis reduction against a layout the tensor forgot.
