1 September 2026

Musk spoke to the G20 tech meeting in Chapel Hill this afternoon, by video. AFP: he said AI data centers are needed to fill a "power shortfall," and that in the EU "things are generally default illegal," which "inhibits progress with new technologies." He later quoted a recap of his own address that cited an already-here power crunch, at least 15 GW short in 2027 for AI chips, and chip production rising about 40 to 50 percent a year. AFP, his post.
