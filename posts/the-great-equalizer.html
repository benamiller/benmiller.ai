Evening brief · 2 September 2026

The great equalizer

2 September 2026

Biggest today was Day 2 of the G20 Innovation Ministerial in Chapel Hill.

Huang, with Commerce Secretary Lutnick: AI infrastructure is like water, roads, electricity, and the internet, "the great equalizer." Don't regulate theoretical or hypothetical harm, regulate actual and pragmatic harm. Mass job wipeout is "nonsense." He told CNBC he hadn't discussed forthcoming semiconductor tariffs at the meeting.

Altman, lunch fireside with Lutnick: country-level AI adoption is "non-negotiable," like skipping electricity a century ago. He predicted "the greatest boom in entrepreneurship" small businesses have seen. On cyber: "some things are going to go very wrong with cybersecurity unless people act quite urgently."

Tom Brown of Anthropic, also with Lutnick: he "really loved" Trump's Truth Social post on data centers, and framed them as jobs, lower taxes, and designs that bring more power onto the grid.

Tesla / Grok: write-ups today on the Summer Update voice controls. A community test logged about 116 of 170 spoken commands hands-free. Expanded hardware control is AMD Ryzen only; Intel Atom cars are locked out. The firmware started earlier. Today's news is the coverage.

Quiet from Hassabis, Karpathy, Ilya, and Dario personally. Morning already covered Astra, Fable/Mythos, Grok 4.7 timing, and SafeMind.
